Welcome to the House of Tau

Find Your Place in the House

Membership in Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. is an opportunity to join a brotherhood built upon the principles of Brotherhood, Scholarship, and Service.

Known as “The House of Tau,” Tau Upsilon Sigma Chapter provides a local home for Sigma men committed to fellowship, personal growth, leadership, and service throughout the geographical area of Clay County, Florida and surrounding areas.

Interested in Phi Beta Sigma?

Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. welcomes qualified men of all backgrounds who share a commitment to Brotherhood, Scholarship, and Service. Membership is not limited by race, ethnicity, or national origin.

We invite men interested in membership in Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. to connect with the Tau Upsilon Sigma Chapter to learn more about the Fraternity, our local chapter, and the membership process.

Attend one of our informational events or contact us to begin the conversation.
