Tau Upsilon Sigma in Action

From community service and scholarship to brotherhood and fellowship, our gallery captures Tau Upsilon Sigma Chapter serving, connecting, and making an impact throughout Clay County.

Sunrise Sigma Walk

June 6, 2026 • Jacksonville Beach Pier

The brothers of Tau Upsilon Sigma started the day in fellowship with prayer and a sunrise walk along Jacksonville Beach—strengthening the bonds of brotherhood while enjoying time together beyond our service in the community.

3rd Annual Blue & White Family Day Picnic

May 30, 2026 • Moody Park • Orange Park, Florida

Tau Upsilon Sigma brought together brothers, families, friends, and community members for our 3rd Annual Blue & White Family Day Picnic—a day of fellowship, food, fun, and celebration.

The chapter also recognized two graduating Clay County high school seniors, David C. Murphy and Khalon A. Franklin, presenting each with $1,500 in combined scholarship support through Tau Upsilon Sigma Chapter and the Dr. Cornelius D. Jones Literacy Scholarship.

Health Fair & Food Drive

May 16, 2026 • Vera Francis Hall Park • Green Cove Springs, Florida

Tau Upsilon Sigma partnered with the Florida Department of Health in Clay County and LS Ministries Community Outreach to host a free community Health Fair & Food Drive focused on supporting the health and well-being of Clay County families.

Community members received free food, health education and resources, and drug education, bringing together service and wellness to address needs within our community.

2026 Southern Regional Conference

April 21–25, 2026 • Miami, Florida

Brothers of Tau Upsilon Sigma joined Sigma Men from across the Southern Region for the 2026 Southern Regional Conference in Miami, Florida.

The conference provided opportunities for brotherhood, leadership development, organizational business, training, and fellowship, while strengthening connections across the region and advancing the mission of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc.

2026 Joint Florida State Conference

February 27–March 1, 2026 • Orlando, Florida

Brothers of Tau Upsilon Sigma joined members of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. and Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Inc. from across Florida for the 2026 Joint Florida State Conference in Orlando.

The weekend brought together brotherhood, sisterhood, leadership development, training, fellowship, and networking, while strengthening connections throughout the Blue and White family and advancing our shared commitment to service.

2026 MLK Day Celebration

January 19, 2026 • Saint James AME Church • Orange Park, Florida

Tau Upsilon Sigma Chapter continued its annual tradition of service at the MLK Day Celebration hosted by Saint James AME Church, with chapter brothers serving as ushers during the celebration.

Dressed in our signature blue blazers, the brothers welcomed guests and supported the church during its annual ecumenical worship celebration honoring the life and legacy of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.

2026 marked Tau Upsilon Sigma’s third year supporting this community celebration through service.
